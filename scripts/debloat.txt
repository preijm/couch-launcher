# Junk to switch off on a Sony BRAVIA 4K VH2 (Google TV, Android 12), used by: scripts/tv.sh debloat
# Taken from Clean Your TV's tested list for this model:
# https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/blob/main/examples/sony-bravia-4k-vh2/debloat.txt
# Packages are disabled, not uninstalled: scripts/tv.sh debloat-undo turns them back on.
# Put a # in front of a line to keep that app. A package the TV doesn't have is skipped.
#
# Left alone on purpose: HDMI/tuner (com.sony.dtv.tvinput.*, tvx, tvapi), Bravia Sync, com.mediatek.*,
# firmware updates, Google Assistant, Chromecast built-in, Bluetooth.
# Also kept: com.sonyericsson.idd.agent and com.sony.dtv.sonybugreportsys; on this model disabling
# either one broke Ethernet through a USB adapter.
